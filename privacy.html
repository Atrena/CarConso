Privacy policy

CarConso · last updated October 2, 2026 · Version française

In short

CarConso does not collect, transmit or sell any data. There is no account, no advertising, no usage analytics and no server.

Data you enter

The vehicles, fill-ups, expenses and settings you enter are stored only on your device (app or browser storage). They only leave the device when you decide so:

  • when you export a backup or an Excel file and choose where to save it or whom to send it to;
  • in the Android app, through Android's automatic backup: if it is turned on in your phone settings, Google backs up the app's data to your Google Drive account, encrypted, to restore it if you reinstall the app or switch phones. This service is run by Google under its own terms; CarConso has no access to it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any sensitive permission: no location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Deletion

You can erase all your data from Settings › Data › Erase everything, or by uninstalling the app.

Contact and source code

CarConso's code is public: github.com/Atrena/CarConso. For any question, open an issue on that page.
